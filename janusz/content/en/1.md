# 1. Introduction

The resistance of a piezoresistor depends on temperature as $R(T) = R_0 (1 + \alpha T)$, where $\alpha$ is expressed in [1/°C] [3, 5].

$$
\frac{\Delta R}{R} = \pi_l \sigma_l + \pi_t \sigma_t
$$
